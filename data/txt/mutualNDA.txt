rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; (d) is disclosed by the Discloser to a third party 
without a duty of confidentiality on the third party; (e) is independently developed by the Recipient; or (f) is available to the public under 
operation of Law.

10.   Company confirms that the confidential information it discloses does not contain export control-listed technology or technical data

University's Export Control Officer beforehand in writing. Company agrees not to provide any export control-listed information to 
Scientist, or others at Stanford University, without the written agreement of Stanford's Export Control Officer.
